Good to know

A few common questions

Who do you work with?

We work with organizations and teams, practitioners and facilitators, and community groups. If you hold space for others in any form, there is likely a way we can support you.

What happens on a discovery call?

We listen. We will ask about your community, what feels alive and what you are reaching for. By the end, you will have a clearer sense of a possible path forward, and the choice of next steps stays fully yours.

Do you work in person or online?

Both. We facilitate gatherings, trainings, and healing spaces in person and online, and we will recommend the format that best serves your people.

How do we get started?

Book a discovery call using the calendar above, or send an email. That first conversation is the doorway in.
