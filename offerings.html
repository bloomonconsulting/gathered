Offerings

What we offer

Each engagement is shaped around your goals. Start with the service that fits, and we build from there.

Community

Building community

We help you build and sustain community.

We design the gatherings, rhythms, and relationships that help people show up, stay, and connect. This fits teams building culture, leaders launching a program, and groups deepening existing bonds.

This can look like: gathering design and facilitation, community rhythms and rituals, belonging and onboarding practices, and coaching for the people who lead your community.

Holding space

Containers for holding space

We build the structures that keep a group safe and steady.

We create shared agreements, clear roles, and thoughtful openings and closings, along with a plan for hard moments, so a group can handle honest conversation.

This can look like: designing group agreements and norms, facilitation frameworks, meeting and retreat containers, and support for holding conflict and repair well.

Healing

Facilitating healing

We facilitate circles and healing spaces.

We facilitate reflection, connection, and repair, grounded in care and consent. This fits teams in hard seasons, communities tending shared wounds, and groups reconnecting.

This can look like: healing circles, restorative and community-care sessions, reflective spaces for staff and leaders, and rituals for grief, transition, and celebration.
