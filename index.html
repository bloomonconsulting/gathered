Why Gathered

Finding the community we need has not always been easy.

Our lives are shaped by our relationships, with ourselves, with the people we love, and with the communities we are part of. We all deserve spaces where we can show up as we are, be met with care, and feel a sense of belonging.

Think of Gathered as a container for curiosity, connection, and growth.
