About

About Gathered

Gathered provides community building, facilitation, and healing-centered training for organizations and facilitators.

Why we started

Why Gathered exists

Gathered started to help organizations and facilitators build community and support healing with more skill and support.

Many teams pour into others while carrying their own load with little support.

We provide the training, facilitation, and structures that make this work more sustainable and effective.

Mission

We equip organizations and facilitators to build community and facilitate healing.

We give organizations, practitioners, and community groups the practices and training to build community and move from trauma-informed to healing-centered practice.

Values

Our values

Wholeness

We see people as whole

We build on people's strengths, culture, and wisdom.

Safety

Safety comes first

We tend to physical, emotional, and relational safety so people can take part fully.

Consent

We move at the pace of trust

People choose their own depth. We invite gently and honor every yes and no.

Joy

Healing makes room for joy

We hold space for grief and for joy, because both belong in the work.
