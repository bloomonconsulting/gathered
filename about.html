About Gathered

We help the people who build community do it with skill and care.

Gathered grew from a simple conviction. Communities grow and heal when the people leading them have the right tools, training, and support. Our work is to equip organizations and facilitators to create that.

Our story

Care that reaches toward wholeness

So much of the work of care stops at managing harm. Gathered began with a bigger question. What would it look like to help people and communities move beyond surviving and toward thriving?

We saw teams and leaders pouring themselves into others while carrying their own weight in silence. We saw beautiful missions held together by people running near empty. And we saw how much shifts when a group learns to hold space for one another with skill and care.

So we built a practice that helps people gather with intention, hold space that stays steady, and grow cultures where healing belongs at the center.

Our purpose

To equip organizations and facilitators to build community-focused spaces and facilitate healing.

We give organizations, practitioners, and community groups the practices, containers, and confidence to create community-centered spaces on purpose, and to move from trauma-informed care toward healing-centered practice.

What guides us

The values underneath the work

Wholeness

We see people as whole

Everyone carries strengths, culture, and wisdom alongside their wounds. We build on what is already alive in people and honor the wholeness they bring.

Safety

Safety comes first

Real work happens when people feel safe. We tend to physical, emotional, and relational safety so participants can arrive fully and stay present.

Consent

We move at the pace of trust

People choose their own depth. We lead with gentle invitation, and we honor the yes and the no with equal respect.

Joy

Healing makes room for joy

Wholeness includes celebration, rest, and delight. We hold space for grief and for joy, because both belong in the work of becoming well.
