Training

From trauma-informed to healing-centered.

Understanding trauma is a beginning. Healing-centered practice is where the work grows toward wholeness. Our training helps your team make that shift with clarity, skill, and care.

Bring this to your team

The shift

Two questions, one big difference

Trauma-informed practice asks, what happened to you. It builds awareness of how pain shapes behavior, and it helps us stop causing further harm. Healing-centered practice keeps that wisdom and reaches further. It asks, what do you need to become well, and it centers strengths, culture, relationships, and hope.

Where many start

Trauma-informed

Recognizes the impact of trauma on people and behavior.
Focuses on safety and avoiding re-harm.
Centers the wound and the individual.
Asks, what happened to you.

Where we grow

Healing-centered

Sees people as whole, with strengths and wisdom.
Focuses on well-being, agency, and thriving.
Centers culture, relationships, and community.
Asks, what helps you become well.

Foundations

Build a shared language for trauma-informed and healing-centered practice, and see clearly what changes when you make the move.

Practice

Culture

Design practices that honor identity, community, and agency, so care builds toward wholeness for everyone involved.

Care

Name and prevent burnout with practices that keep the people who hold others resourced and well.

Repair

Approach rupture and conflict as a doorway to repair, with tools that turn hard moments into rebuilt trust.

Integration

Make it stick

Leave with concrete next steps and rhythms to weave healing-centered practice into daily work.
