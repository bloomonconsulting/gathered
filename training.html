Training

From trauma-informed to healing-centered.

Our training helps your team move from trauma-informed to healing-centered practice.

Bring this to your team

The shift

What changes

Trauma-informed practice asks what happened to someone. Healing-centered practice focuses on what helps them become well, centering strengths, culture, relationships, and hope.

Where many start

Trauma-informed

Recognizes the impact of trauma on people and behavior.
Focuses on safety and avoiding re-harm.
Centers the wound and the individual.
Asks, what happened to you.

Where we grow

Healing-centered

Sees people as whole, with strengths and wisdom.
Focuses on well-being, agency, and thriving.
Centers culture, relationships, and community.
Asks, what helps you become well.

What your team will learn

What we cover

Foundations

Understand the shift

Build a shared language for trauma-informed and healing-centered practice, and see clearly what changes when you make the move.

Practice

Hold space with skill

Learn to create containers, guide hard conversations, and stay steady and present when emotion rises in a room.

Culture

Center strengths and culture

Design practices that honor identity, community, and agency, so care builds toward wholeness for everyone involved.

Care

Tend to the helpers

Name and prevent burnout with practices that keep the people who hold others resourced and well.

Repair

Move through conflict

Approach rupture and conflict as a doorway to repair, with tools that turn hard moments into rebuilt trust.

Integration

Make it stick

Leave with concrete next steps and rhythms to weave healing-centered practice into daily work.

Formats

Training formats

Workshops

Half-day and full-day sessions that give your team a shared foundation and practical tools they can use right away.

Learning series

A guided journey across several sessions, so practice has time to deepen and become part of how your team works.

Retreats & intensives

Immersive time away to reset, reconnect, and practice healing-centered leadership together.

Every training is tailored to your team. We will shape the right depth and format together.
